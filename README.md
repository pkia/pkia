### EV

Right now: the signal copier runs live at a deliberately small size, and the work is proving the parts that touch real money — broker-side stops, a rejection path that used to leave a phantom trade, and a break-even rule measured on the channel's own signals before it was wired in. On the Pi, the chaos drill now treats a sweep in flight as proof of life rather than a dead timer, and the CS2 coach's plugin flags reach the stored review report.
